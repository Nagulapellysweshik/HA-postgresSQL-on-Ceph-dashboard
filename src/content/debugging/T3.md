# T3, planned switchover: debugging

The full investigation, with screenshots and logs, is in **[CASE-STUDY.md](CASE-STUDY.md)**. Summary:

## Blockers hit

### B1. Database switchover 7.6 s, client outage 30 s
- **Diagnosis chain:** client errors (`server login has been failing, cached error`) → operator events (database ready at 7.6 s) → **PgBouncer log** (two 15 s cached-error windows) → Postgres log (`FATAL password authentication failed`, `DETAIL … scram-sha-256`).
- **Root cause:** PgBouncer `server_login_retry` (default 15 s) caches a failed server login and rejects every client for that long. It happened twice: `connect failed`, then a SCRAM rejection.
- **Fix:** `server_login_retry: "1"`. Verified with t3-r2: **8.0 s**.
- **Evidence:** `results/raw/t3-r{1,2}.pooler-rw.grouped.txt`, `.log.gz`, `t3-r1.new-primary-auth.txt`; screenshots `t3-r1-…`, `t3-r2-…`.

### B2. SCRAM rejection of pre-switchover PgBouncer sessions (open)
- Reproduced in 2 of 2 runs. The role's verifier is unchanged (no `ALTER ROLE` in the log). Contained to < 1 s by B1's fix; the mechanism isn't determined yet.
