# T10, HAProxy health-check timing: debugging

## Blockers hit

### B1. Identical "detection" times for every setting: a measurement artifact
- **Evidence:** `results/raw/t10-a-haproxy.txt` (1.617 / 1.445 / 1.438 s) vs `t10-a.haproxy-live.txt` and screenshot `t10-a-haproxy-timing-live.png`.
- **Diagnosis:** the live view showed `UP 2/3 → DOWN` taking ~4 s at 1 s × 3, and DNS removal before DOWN at 2 s × 5. That's inconsistent with identical script numbers. Reading the script: `grep -q "$IP:UP"` vs HAProxy's `"$IP:5432:UP"` **never matches**, so the loop exited at once and timed one `kubectl exec` (~1.5 s).
- **Fix:** port-aware match (`"$IP:5432:UP( |$)"`), deletion timestamp logged, final state printed.
- **Lesson:** cross-check measurements against an independent view.
