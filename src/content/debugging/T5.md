# T5, Ceph OSD loss: debugging

## Blockers hit

### B1. The impact came when the disk returned
- **Evidence:** `results/raw/t5-r1.timeline.csv`, `t5-r1.ceph-live.txt`, screenshot `t5-r1-osd-loss-4-pane-live.png`.
- **Diagnosis:** per-second throughput aligned with the Ceph status lines:
  - 1 s dip at the loss (re-peering);
  - 3 min at full throughput while degraded;
  - **2.7 s stall at 08:59:10** (rejoin peering);
  - **37 s at 35%** (`active+recovering`, degraded objects falling 3.1% → 0).
- **Root cause:** recovery/backfill I/O competes with client I/O on the same OSDs and network.
- **Remedy (not applied yet):** `osd_mclock_profile=high_client_ops`, or limit `osd_max_backfills` / `osd_recovery_max_active`; bring disks back off-peak.

### B2. Latent damage, discovered 70 minutes later in T11
- Pausing and resuming the Rook operator in this test is the prime suspect for the CSI `ClientProfile` later being missing and `ceph-csi-config` being `[]`.
- **The test passed; new PVC mounts were broken afterwards.** See [T11](../t11-minor-upgrade/DEBUGGING.md).
- **Lesson:** after any fault injection, re-run the storage path check (step 06: PVC bind + mount + snapshot) before declaring success.
