# T7, connection storm: debugging

## Blockers hit

### B1. The DBA was locked out during the storm
- **Evidence:** screenshot `t7-a-connection-storm-direct-vs-pooled.png` (pane 3); the script's mid-run sample printed an empty value.
- **Symptom:** the monitoring `psql` (superuser, local socket) → `FATAL: sorry, too many clients already`.
- **Root cause:** `superuser_reserved_connections` slots were consumed (CNPG's own connections plus the storm). Nothing was left for an admin.
- **Remedy:** raise the reserved slots / `reserved_connections` for a monitoring role (PG 16+); monitor from a path that doesn't compete for slots; keep application traffic behind the pooler.

### B2. Only 57 direct clients, not ~95
- **Diagnosis:** the backend count at idle was **40**: PgBouncer's idle server connections, holding slots before the storm began.
- **Lesson:** pool connections count against `max_connections`.

### B3. Connect timeouts as well as refusals
- 6 917 connects hit the 2 s `connect_timeout`: Postgres was too busy forking and rejecting backends to accept new ones quickly. Client backoff kept it bounded.
